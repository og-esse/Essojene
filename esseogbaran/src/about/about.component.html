A little more about me

About Me

I’m currently a Senior Software Engineer and creative technologist who loves problem solving. I enjoy turning complex problems into simple, intuitive interfaces. I get a lot of gratitude from seeing my work come to life in the browser and make an impact in the real world.

I also work across backend and platform layers, designing contract-first REST APIs, middleware orchestration, service integrations, authenticated endpoints, and resilient downstream-call patterns for enterprise systems.

My journey started at Wilfrid Laurier University where I studied Computer Science and participated in a Hackathon. I worked on a connected glove that combined communication, warmth, and contactless payments. This project won top prize at a fashion tech hackathon. Since then, I’ve brought that same mix of creativity and practicality into my real life work.

At RBC, I’ve shipped tools that directly impact thousands of users: from a digital mortgage renewal experience that boosted conversions, to an investments account flow that modernized a legacy journey, to an Embedded Advice tax-savings calculator that brings real-time financial guidance into self-serve digital flows, to design system work used across the bank to speed up development and improve consistency.

What keeps me going is the unknown. A problem I haven't solved before, a system behaving in a way I don't yet understand. That's what pulls me into production issues: digging through logs, tracing requests across services, chasing "why" until I hit the root cause. However, I get just as much from taking a rough idea and building it into something real, a working experience someone can use.

My toolkit includes Angular, React, Node.js, REST APIs, GraphQL, and Spring Boot, alongside operational tools like Splunk, SQL, and Cypress. I work closely with product managers, security teams, and backend engineers to ship scalable, maintainable software rather than just one-off UI, and I’ve increasingly taken ownership of backend work by designing and building APIs myself.

Outside of work, I run creative brands called JNWRLD and CYK, where I explore identity and individuality through storytelling, technology, and interactive experiences. You can find them at jnwrld.com and cyk.life.

I like learning new things in my free time. Last year I taught myself how to sew, DJ, and roller skate; this year it’s shaders and game engines. Whether it’s technology or life skills, I tend to go deep and stay curious.

Right now I’m looking for roles where I can combine engineering, investigation, and automation to solve critical problems.

🧩 Little Known Fact

I love solving puzzles and brain teasers. I actually play Sudoku while walking on the treadmill. Multitasking is my default state.
