About Me

I'm currently a Senior Software Engineer at RBC and a creative technologist who loves problem solving. I enjoy figuring out how things work, turning complex problems into simple experiences, and seeing something I've built make an impact in the real world.

My journey started at Wilfrid Laurier University, where I studied Computer Science and helped build a connected glove that combined communication, warmth, and contactless payments. The project won multiple awards at a fashion tech hackathon, and I think that experience shaped how I approach technology today: a mix of curiosity, creativity, and practicality.

At RBC, I've worked on financial applications used by thousands of customers, from mortgage renewals to investment account journeys and tax-savings tools. My work spans frontend, backend, and platform engineering, including API development, service integrations, and debugging complex systems. I've also helped bring AI-assisted development into engineering workflows and participated in early evaluations of GitHub Copilot and Amazon CodeWhisperer.

What keeps me going is the unknown. A problem I haven't solved before, a system behaving in a way I don't understand, or an idea I don't quite know how to build yet. I love digging through logs, tracing requests, and chasing the "why" until something clicks.

AI has completely changed how I explore that curiosity. Tools like Codex have become my coding partners, helping me learn things outside my usual expertise, experiment faster, and turn ideas into working products. I've been exploring Python and machine learning through projects like an FX pricing engine and an MCP model-serving prototype. I'm just as interested in understanding why models work, where they fail, and whether their outputs can be trusted as I am in what they can build.

Outside of RBC, I run creative brands called JNWRLD and CYK, where I explore identity, fashion, storytelling, and interactive technology. They've become my playground for experimenting with ideas and building things end to end. You can find them at jnwrld.com and cyk.life.

I'm also a serial hobby collector. Last year I taught myself how to sew, DJ, and roller skate; this year it's shaders, game engines, and whatever else catches my attention. Whether it's technology or something completely unrelated, I tend to go deep once I'm curious.

Ultimately, I want to keep working on problems that challenge me, combining engineering fundamentals, AI, investigation, and creativity to build things that are genuinely useful.
